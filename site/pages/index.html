Features

Everything you need to keep a clean desktop

Small, fast and focused on one job: putting files where they belong, without surprises.

Preview, then one click

Press Preview first to see which files would move and why. Then Organize now sorts them into category folders in seconds.

Undo anything

Every tidy-up is listed on the History page. Put the whole tidy-up back, or just one file, with the reason it moved shown beside it.

Smart automation

A background watcher files new items on your desktop and Downloads as they arrive. Set quiet hours or pause it any time from the tray icon.

Your rules

Sort by file type, by name (like Screenshot*), by size, by month or year, and move old files away. Drag a file onto the editor to add its type.

Files that never move

Programs, shortcuts, files changed a minute ago and any name or type you protect stay exactly where they are.

Find duplicate files

Finds identical copies, keeps the oldest, and sends only the extras you tick to the Recycle Bin. Nothing is removed without your choice.

Private by design

Everything happens on your PC. No account, no tracking, no uploads. It only asks this website whether a newer version exists.

Easy to read

Use it in English, Hindi or Punjabi, with bigger text or a high-contrast option, and keyboard shortcuts (press F1 for the guide).

Nothing to hunt for

The History page and the log show what moved and where, so you can always find a file again. A small notice appears after an automatic tidy.

How it works

Tidy in three steps

Nothing moves until you press a button. Look first, then tidy, and undo if you change your mind.

  1. Install and open

    Run DeskOrgSetup.exe, then open DeskOrg from your desktop or Start Menu. A welcome screen asks which folders to look after (Desktop, and Downloads if you like) and your language.

  2. Preview, then tidy

    On Tidy up now, press Preview first to see what would move, then Organize now. Not happy? Undo is one click away on the History page.

  3. Automate (optional)

    In Keep it tidy automatically, turn on the watcher so new files are filed from now on. Pause it or set quiet hours whenever you like.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is DeskOrg free?

Yes. DeskOrg is free and released under the MIT licence. There are no ads, accounts or trials.

Does DeskOrg upload my files?

No. Everything runs on your PC. DeskOrg only moves files between folders on your own computer.

Does it delete anything?

No. It moves files into category folders using your rules, and protected files stay where they are. Every tidy-up is listed on the History page and can be undone. The only tool that removes anything is Find duplicate files, and it sends only the extra copies you tick to the Recycle Bin.

Can I undo a tidy-up?

Yes. Open History, press Undo to put a whole tidy-up back, or Details then Put back to return a single file to the folder it came from.

Can it tidy Downloads or other folders?

Yes. Pick Downloads in the welcome screen, or add it (or any folder) under Where files go. Each folder can have its own options, and the watcher follows them live.

Can I change which folder a file type goes to?

Yes. Open Where files go and press Open rules editor. You can sort by type, by name (for example Screenshot*), by size, by month or year, and protect files you never want moved. Your rules are saved in your own Windows profile, so they survive updates, and you can export them to use on another PC.

Do I have to keep it running?

No. Use Organize now whenever you like. Only if you turn on automation does a small background watcher file new items for you; you can pause it from the tray icon or turn it off any time.

Is it available in my language?

The main screens are in English, Hindi and Punjabi. Choose the language on the welcome screen or under Where files go. You can also make the text bigger or use high-contrast text there.

How do I uninstall it?

Open Windows Settings › Apps, find DeskOrg and choose Uninstall. It also removes the background watcher and its startup task. Your organized folders stay where they are.
